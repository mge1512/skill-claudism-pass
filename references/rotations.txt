# Terminology rotations. One synonym set per line, members separated by |.
#
# English rewards variety in prose and punishes it in technical writing. Using
# "check" here and "verify" there makes a reader who parses English as a second
# language stop and ask whether two different things are meant. Pick one member
# per document and keep it.
#
# Reported as a notice, never gated: a document may legitimately use "delete"
# and "remove" for two different operations, and only the author knows.
#
# The sets come from the simple-english skill (ASD-STE100 rules 1.11 and 9.4).
check|verify|confirm|validate
config|configuration|settings|options|preferences
run|execute|invoke|launch
show|display|render|present
delete|remove|drop|destroy
error|issue|problem|failure|fault
start|begin|initiate|commence
build|compile|assemble
enable|activate
folder|directory
