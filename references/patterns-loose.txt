# The copula flourish: a clause that redefines its subject and hedges with a
# trailing "here". Approximated without lookbehind, so "there is a X here" and
# similar legitimate locatives turn up too. Judgment call.

# Testimony verbs (review, 2026-10). A person, a project or a certificate
# authority vouching is literal; a decoder, a checksum or a signature vouching
# is the agency metaphor in banlist.md. Judgment call.
